Abstract

الملخص

This study aims to analyze the security media discourse of the Royal Oman Police and identify its linguistic and media characteristics, as well as its role in combating transnational crime and promoting public security awareness. The study addresses the central question of how security media discourse employs linguistic devices and media strategies to achieve its communicative and preventive objectives within the framework of Omani legislation and relevant international conventions. A descriptive-analytical qualitative approach was adopted, using discourse analysis to examine a purposive sample of official press releases, awareness campaigns, and security media messages issued by the Royal Oman Police concerning transnational crimes, particularly cybercrime, human trafficking, money laundering, and drug trafficking.
The findings reveal that the Royal Oman Police›s security media discourse is characterized by the use of precise legal and security terminology, the predominance of direct informative sentence structures, and the effective use of rhetorical, argumentative, and pragmatic strategies to strengthen persuasion and enhance message credibility. The analysis also demonstrates a clear integration between linguistic and media dimensions through the combination of informative and awareness-raising functions and the effective utilization of digital media platforms to disseminate security messages, build public trust, and reinforce community partnership. Furthermore, the study indicates that the discourse consistently adheres to legal terminology and procedural accuracy, reflecting its close connection with the legal framework governing security work and supporting preventive criminal policy in addressing transnational crime.
