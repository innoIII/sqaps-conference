Research Abstract

ملخص البحث

This study aimed to measure the level of societal awareness regarding transnational crimes among students in grades 11–9 in public schools in the Sultanate of Oman, among the rapid expansion of digital technology use, with growth of cybercrimes and other cross-border offenses. The study adopted a descriptive methodology, using a questionnaire designed to measure societal awareness across three main dimensions: the cognitive dimension, the affective dimension, and the behavioral dimension. The tool was applied to a sample of public-school students.
The results showed that the level of societal awareness among the sample members was high, with an overall arithmetic mean of 2.52 out of 3, corresponding to an estimated rate of %84.1. The behavioral dimension ranked first, followed by the cognitive dimension, and then the affective dimension. This reflects students’ adoption of positive preventive practices, a general awareness of the severity and impact of transnational crimes, as well as attitudes that reject these crimes and sympathize with their victims. Conversely, the results revealed areas requiring further reinforcement, such as deepening detailed knowledge of transnational crime patterns, enhancing personal data protection, increasing student participation in awareness initiatives, and boosting their sense of self-efficacy to contribute to crime prevention.
The study confirms that building societal awareness is not limited to providing students with information; rather, it requires a balanced development of cognitive, affective, and behavioral dimensions to enhance their ability to make safe decisions in the digital environment. The study recommends developing sustainable school awareness programs that include practical scenarios and digital risk simulations, integrating transnational crime concepts into digital education and citizenship curricula, and strengthening partnerships between the Ministry of learning, the Royal Oman Police, and relevant authorities to help establish a culture of prevention, enhance societal security, and support national efforts in combating transnational crimes.
Keywords: Transnational Crimes, Societal Awareness, Cognitive Dimension, Affective Dimension, Behavioral Dimension, Grades 11–9 Students.
